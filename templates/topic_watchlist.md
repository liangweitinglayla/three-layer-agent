# Topic Watchlist — template

Directive 01 reads this file and searches **only** streams marked `on`.
Editing this file is how you steer the system. Set a stream to `off` and it
goes quiet; no other change is needed.

## 1. <Stream name> — on
One or two lines on what belongs in this stream.
Search hints: outlets, recurring terms, primary sources worth checking
directly rather than through coverage.

## 2. <Stream name> — on
As above. Where a stream has a hard rule — something to cover only at a
general level, or a subject to handle carefully — write it here as a
GUARDRAIL line. Directive 03 will enforce it.

## 3. <Stream name> — off
Streams you have paused. Leave them in the file; deleting loses the search
hints you worked out.

## 4. <Essay stream> — on
A non-news stream, if you have one: essays prompted by an anniversary, a
cultural moment, a story that resonates. Directive 01 surfaces at most one
of these per week.
