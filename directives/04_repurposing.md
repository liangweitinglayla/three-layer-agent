# 04 — Repurposing

## Goal
One finished piece becomes several short-form items without becoming filler.

## Rules
- One article yields **3–5 distinct threads**, each making **one** sharp
  claim. Never a summary-thread of the whole article — that is the version
  nobody reads and nobody shares.
- Structure per thread: hook first, evidence with source and date in the
  middle, punch or open question at the end.
- Short-form maps to declared content pillars. If a post fits no pillar,
  it is off-strategy, however good it is.
- **Draft only. Never post, schedule, DM, or log into an account.** The
  writer publishes everything themselves.

## Known failure modes
- Repurposing produces volume, and volume feels like progress. It is not
  the metric. See directive 05.
