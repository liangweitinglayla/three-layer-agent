# 01 — Topic monitoring

## Goal
Produce a weekly digest of what happened in your subject areas, and from it
a short list of pieces actually worth writing.

## Inputs
- `templates/topic_watchlist.md` — your streams, each switched on or off
- The last 7 days of sources

## Steps
1. Read the watchlist. Search **only** streams marked `on`.
2. For each active stream, collect what happened, with source and date.
3. Produce the digest: source + date + one line. No commentary yet.
4. From the digest, propose 3–5 candidate pieces. For each: the claim, why
   now, and what evidence already exists.
5. **Stop and ask which to write.** Do not begin drafting unprompted.

## Rules
- A stream set to `off` is not searched. The watchlist is the interface;
  editing it is how the writer steers the system.
- Digest and candidates are separate outputs. Being informed is useful even
  in a week when nothing gets written.
- At most one non-news candidate per week (an essay prompt — an
  anniversary, a cultural moment, a resonant story).
- Never propose a topic already covered. Check the drafts folder first.

## Known failure modes
- Search result snippets are frequently truncated. A claim that rests on a
  snippet must be checked against the full source before it reaches a draft.
- Aggregators republish each other. Three hits are often one source.
